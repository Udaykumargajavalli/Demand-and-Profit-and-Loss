## Overview

The project has four primary stakeholder groups.

## Implementation Details

| Stakeholder | Interest | Responsibility | Communication |
|---|---|---|---|
| Data team | Reliable and approved forecasting inputs | Supply historical demand, volume, macroeconomic, CPI, inflation, and other data dependencies | Data availability and quality reviews |
| ML leadership | Model quality, governance, and strategic direction | Review model approach, evaluation, MAPE reporting, retraining, and champion selection | Model governance and leadership reviews |
| Data analyst team | Forecast and P&L reporting for leadership | Consume forecast outputs and use them for reporting for the Leadership Team (LT) | Reporting and LT review packs |
| Zone team | Country and zone-level planning outcomes | Review forecasts, assess deviations, and use outputs for operational and strategic planning | Zone and country forecast reviews |
