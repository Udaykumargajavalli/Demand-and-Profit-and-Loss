## Overview

Demand and P&L Forecasting is an end-to-end machine learning platform designed to forecast demand and key Profit and Loss metrics for Beer and Non-Alcoholic Beverage (NAB) product categories in a consumer-products planning environment.

The solution combines statistical forecasting, machine learning, external economic indicators, and automated data pipelines to produce consistent, explainable, and scalable forecasts. It supports executive planning and granular operational analysis.

Forecasting features include seasonality and calendar signals, temperature and weather variables, pricing and commercial details, and macroeconomic indicators. These inputs support both demand and normalized P&L forecasting across monthly planning periods.

## Purpose

The platform provides an independent, data-driven forecast that complements regional planning-team forecasts while standardizing recurring forecast production across markets and product categories.

## Forecast Scope

- Forecast demand and five primary P&L components.
- Calculate Net Revenue and Marginal Contribution from forecast outputs.
- Support top-down and bottom-up forecasting.
- Model normalized per-hectoliter values to reduce volume-driven distortion.
- Use regression, tree-based, time-series, and deep-learning models.
- Automate training, scoring, retraining, and accuracy reporting.
- Generate recurring forecasts from the current quarter through year-end.

## Typical Forecast Outputs

| Output | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Volume forecast | Demand planning and supply visibility |
| Gross Turnover forecast | Price and volume-related revenue planning |
| Excise forecast | Tax and duty planning |
| Discount forecast | Commercial deduction planning |
| Industrial cost forecast | Variable production cost planning |
| Logistics cost forecast | Distribution cost planning |
| Net Revenue | Derived financial result |
| Marginal Contribution | Derived contribution result |
