## Analysis Method

Forecast deviations were investigated across:

- Country and product category.
- Brand, channel, and SKU.
- P&L component.
- Forecast horizon.
- Model forecast versus business forecast.
- Volume, price, discount, excise, industrial cost, and logistics cost.

The objective was to distinguish data issues, model limitations, external changes, exceptional events, and business assumptions.
