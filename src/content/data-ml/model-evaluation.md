## Recommended Metrics

- Weighted Absolute Percentage Error.
- Mean Absolute Percentage Error.
- Mean Absolute Error.
- Root Mean Squared Error.
- Forecast bias.
- Directional accuracy.
- Accuracy within an agreed tolerance.
- Absolute and percentage deviation from actuals.

## Evaluation Dimensions

- Country.
- Product category.
- Brand.
- Channel.
- SKU.
- Forecast horizon.
- P&L component.
- Top-down versus bottom-up.
- Model forecast versus business forecast.

> **Note:** We calculated and reported the forecasting metrics required for model evaluation, but MAPE (Mean Absolute Percentage Error) was the primary metric reported as model accuracy.
