## Overview

The platform combines SQL Server sources, flat files, and Azure-hosted datasets.

## Source Inventory

- SQL Server databases.
- Flat files stored in Azure Storage.
- Historical demand and sales data.
- Beer and NAB product-category and SKU data.
- Product and SKU hierarchies.
- Price and discount information.
- Excise and tax information.
- Industrial and logistics costs.
- Weather variables.
- Temperature variables.
- Macroeconomic indicators.
- Consumer Price Index (CPI).
- Inflation indicators.
- Industry indicators.
- Calendar and monthly seasonality features.
- Exceptional-event flags.

External macroeconomic information, temperature and weather observations, and calendar and seasonal indicators helped the models respond to changing market conditions for Beer and NAB forecasting.
