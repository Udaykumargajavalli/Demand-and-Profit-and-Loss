## Overview

Forecast explainability was supported through feature selection, model feature importance, and deviation reporting.

## Explainability Practices

- Review the variables driving demand and P&L movements.
- Use feature importance to identify influential predictors.
- Separate volume effects from price, tax, discount, and cost-rate effects.
- Explain large forecast movements and exceptional events.
- Compare model forecasts against business forecasts.
- Report hierarchy-level accuracy and deviations.
