## Overview

Exploratory analysis examined historical behavior, hierarchy differences, seasonal patterns, external drivers, and abnormal observations.

## Key Analysis Areas

- Monthly demand and P&L trends.
- Differences across Argentina, Chile, Peru, and Uruguay.
- CPI and inflation patterns by country.
- Beer versus NAB differences.
- Monthly, seasonal, and holiday patterns.
- Temperature and weather response.
- Pricing and commercial-deduction relationships.
- Seasonality and high-sales periods.
- Country, category, channel, brand, and SKU differences.
- Price, discount, tax, and cost relationships.
- Weather and macroeconomic correlations.
- Industry indicators.
- Exceptional-event effects.
- Outlier concentration and structural changes.
- Feature redundancy and multicollinearity.
