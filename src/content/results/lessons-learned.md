## Lessons

- Business context is as important as algorithm selection.
- Normalizing financial targets improves cross-market stability.
- External variables are useful only when future availability is reliable.
- Outliers should be investigated before removal or replacement.
- Time-aware validation is essential for realistic performance.
- Accuracy should be reported at the hierarchy used for business decisions.
- Simple models provide strong baselines and explainability.
- Configuration-driven pipelines improve reuse but require validation and governance.
- Forecast monitoring must continue after deployment.
- Forecasts create more value when stakeholders understand drivers and limitations.
