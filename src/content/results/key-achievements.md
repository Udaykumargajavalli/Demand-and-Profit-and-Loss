## Implementation Details

- Automated a multi-stage demand and P&L forecasting pipeline.
- Supported both Beer and Non-Alcoholic Beverage (NAB) forecasting.
- Added country coverage for Argentina, Chile, Peru, and Uruguay.
- Integrated CPI and inflation with pricing and macroeconomic indicators.
- Integrated seasonality, calendar, temperature, pricing, and macroeconomic indicators.
- Integrated top-down and bottom-up forecasting across country and detailed hierarchies.
- Standardized per-hectoliter P&L modeling and total-value reconstruction.
- Added weather, economic, industry, calendar, seasonal, and exceptional-event features.
- Evaluated regression, tree-based, time-series, and deep-learning model families.
- Implemented training, scoring, retraining, accuracy reporting, and Azure Storage publication.
- Delivered an independent forecast for stakeholder comparison.
