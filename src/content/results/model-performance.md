## Performance Summary

The platform reported approximately 85% historical accuracy for country-level demand and P&L components.

Before this result is presented as a formal KPI, publication materials should define:

- Accuracy formula.
- Evaluation period.
- Included hierarchies.
- P&L component and demand weighting.
- Forecast horizon.
- Comparison to business or consensus forecasts.
